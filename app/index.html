The science behind it

  1. Resting energy (BMR): the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. Mifflin et al., 1990, Am J Clin Nutr 51:241–7. A systematic review found it the most accurate common equation for non-obese and obese adults: Frankenfield et al., 2005, J Am Diet Assoc. If you enter body fat %, the app uses Katch–McArdle (370 + 21.6 × lean mass) instead.
  2. Why targets adapt: the body's energy needs fall as weight falls, so the old fixed "7,700 kcal = 1 kg" rule overestimates long-term loss. Hall et al., 2011, The Lancet 378:826–37, which is also the model behind the NIH Body Weight Planner. MacroLog works backwards from your logged intake and weight trend to estimate your real expenditure each week.
  3. Safe rate of loss: 0.5–1.0% of body weight per week to keep muscle. Helms, Aragon & Fitschen, 2014, JISSN 11:20. In elite athletes, slower loss (~0.7%/wk) kept more lean mass than faster loss (~1.4%/wk): Garthe et al., 2011, IJSNEM 21:97–104.
  4. Rate of gain: about 0.25–0.5% of body weight per week limits fat gain while building muscle. Iraki et al., 2019, Sports 7:154.
  5. Protein: gains in muscle from resistance training level off around 1.6 g/kg/day (95% CI up to ~2.2). Morton et al., 2018, Br J Sports Med 52:376–84. The ISSN recommends 1.4–2.0 g/kg, and more during a deficit: Jäger et al., 2017, JISSN 14:20. When cutting, 2.3–3.1 g/kg of lean mass is recommended (Helms 2014). If your BMI is 30 or more, protein is based on your target weight so the number isn't inflated.
  6. Fat and fibre: the Acceptable Macronutrient Distribution Range for fat is 20–35% of energy (US Institute of Medicine, 2005), and Helms 2014 suggests 15–30% during contest prep. The app never goes below 0.6 g/kg. The fibre guide is 14 g per 1,000 kcal (Dietary Guidelines for Americans). Carbohydrate fills the remaining calories.
  7. Food data: Food Standards Australia New Zealand: Australian Food Composition Database (AFCD) Release 3 and AUSNUT 2011–13. USDA National Nutrient Database for Standard Reference (SR28). Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB), 2024, Curr Dev Nutr, which has 1,014 Indian recipes built on ICMR-NIN IFCT 2017. Carbohydrates are available carbs, meaning fibre is excluded, which is the Australian labelling convention.
This app is for education and self-tracking. It isn't medical advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor or an Accredited Practising Dietitian before changing your diet.
